Our story

A corner of Iloilo to slow down.

“Sulod” means come in in Hiligaynon, and that’s the whole idea. We roast in small batches, partner with Philippine coffee growers, and pair every cup with something baked fresh each morning.

Whether you’re walking the Esplanade, between classes, or finishing a batchoy run in Jaro, there’s a seat with your name on it.
